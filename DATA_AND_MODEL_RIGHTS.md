# Rights and attribution by component

The root MIT license applies to author-owned research software and its technical
documentation, including the independent auditors and path-portable wrappers.
It does not replace any embedded upstream license. No blanket MIT license is
assigned to the complete data/model bundle or manuscript PDFs.

- SciFact annotations: upstream CC BY 4.0; abstracts: upstream S2ORC ODC-By 1.0;
  upstream code: Apache-2.0. See `UPSTREAM_NOTICES/SciFact_LICENSE.md` and attribution.
- GSM8K, SVAMP and StrategyQA: preserved respective upstream notices apply to
  reused/transformed records; transformations are described in protocols/notices.
- Qwen base/tokenizer/configuration components and Qwen-derived specialist
  adapters: Apache-2.0, with original license texts preserved under
  `UPSTREAM_NOTICES/`. Published adapters are the exact registered experimental
  LoRA deltas, not full model distributions. The three poisoned adapters are
  explicitly designated experimental attack controls.
- Historical Llama dependencies: official Llama terms; no Llama base weights
  are redistributed. Original missing-weight limitations remain.
- Original manuscript and author-generated results retain author ownership;
  benchmark-derived source text retains upstream terms. The public deposit
  permits inspection/reconstruction and provides the accompanying author software.

The archived review-stage statement that no author-code redistribution license
was assigned records its earlier status. This public release supplies the MIT
license for author-owned software prospectively; it does not retroactively
change upstream ownership or claim a license for unavailable components.
